The Phelan Green development Die Phelan Green-ontwikkeling

A large green-hydrogen fuel plant and a 1.6 GW solar farm are proposed on the farmland just north of Hopefield. Nothing has been decided. This site gathers what is publicly known about the project — in plain language and in detail — and gives residents a way to take part. 'n Groot groen-waterstof brandstof-aanleg en 'n 1.6 GW sonplaas word voorgestel op die plaasgrond net noord van Hopefield. Niks is besluit nie. Hierdie webwerf versamel wat openbaar bekend is oor die projek — in eenvoudige taal en in besonderhede — en gee inwoners 'n manier om deel te neem.

The nearest deadline is 8 October 2026. The solar farm's Final Scoping Report was released on 8 September 2026, and written comments go to the consultant, percy@ecocompliance.co.za, by 8 October. Printed copies are at the Hopefield, Saldanha and Langebaan libraries. This is only the end of the scoping stage — a fuller study stage, with another comment round, still follows. How to comment is set out in the briefing.

Die naaste sperdatum is 8 Oktober 2026. Die sonplaas se Finale Bestekverslag is op 8 September 2026 vrygestel, en skriftelike kommentaar gaan na die konsultant, percy@ecocompliance.co.za, teen 8 Oktober. Gedrukte afskrifte is by die Hopefield-, Saldanha- en Langebaan-biblioteke. Dit is net die einde van die bestek-fase — 'n voller studie-fase, met nog 'n kommentaar-ronde, volg steeds. Hoe om kommentaar te lewer word in die oorsig uiteengesit.

What this is

Wat dit is

Run by residents, for residents

Deur inwoners, vir inwoners

This site is put together by a group of Hopefield residents, working alongside the West Coast Environmental Protection Association (WCEPA). It is not run by the developer, the municipality or any political party, and it is not funded by anyone. Everything here is drawn from documents that are already public — the developer's own reports, the written responses from government bodies, and news coverage.

Hierdie webwerf word saamgestel deur 'n groep Hopefield-inwoners, saam met die Weskus Omgewingsbeskermingsvereniging (WCEPA). Dit word nie deur die ontwikkelaar, die munisipaliteit of enige politieke party bestuur nie, en dit word deur niemand befonds nie. Alles hier kom uit dokumente wat reeds openbaar is — die ontwikkelaar se eie verslae, die skriftelike reaksies van staatsliggame, en nuusdekking.

The aim is simple: that everyone in the town can find out what is going on and have a real say in it — not just the people who make it to meetings.

Die doel is eenvoudig: dat almal in die dorp kan uitvind wat aangaan en 'n werklike sê daarin kan hê — nie net die mense wat by vergaderings uitkom nie.
