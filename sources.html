Where the briefing comes from Waar die oorsig vandaan kom

Every figure in the residents' briefing is drawn from a document that is already public. This page lists them, so anyone can check the briefing against the originals. Elke syfer in die inwoner-oorsig is uit 'n dokument geneem wat reeds openbaar is. Hierdie bladsy lys hulle, sodat enigiemand die oorsig teen die oorspronklikes kan toets.

Most of the documents below are collected in one place — the resident-run site whyhopefield.co.za, where the scoping reports, specialist studies, public notices and correspondence can be downloaded. This page is a guide to what those documents are; the briefing quotes them, and where they disagree with each other the briefing says so. Die meeste van die dokumente hieronder is op een plek versamel — die inwoner-bestuurde webwerf whyhopefield.co.za, waar die bestekverslae, spesialisstudies, openbare kennisgewings en korrespondensie afgelaai kan word. Hierdie bladsy is 'n gids tot wat daardie dokumente is; die oorsig haal hulle aan, en waar hulle mekaar weerspreek, sê die oorsig dit.

The case for the project

Die saak vir die projek

The evidence behind the possible benefits — national policy, the developer's own track record elsewhere, and the renewable-energy programme's community-trust model nationally.

Die bewyse agter die moontlike voordele — nasionale beleid, die ontwikkelaar se eie rekord elders, en die hernubare-energie-program se gemeenskapstrust-model nasionaal.

  • South Africa's Hydrogen Society Roadmap (Department of Science and Innovation, 2021); the Green Hydrogen Commercialisation Strategy (2022); and the Just Energy Transition Investment Plan 2023–2027 — the national policy basis for treating green hydrogen as a strategic priority, including projected national emissions and jobs figures.
  • SAPVIA, “Phelan Green Group Celebrates Public Health Breakthrough in De Aar Through Renewable Energy Investment” — and related industry/PR coverage (pv magazine, SolarQuarter, Green Building Africa, PRWeb) of the De Aar and Loeriesfontein solar plants' construction employment and community-development spending. Independently reported, but note it draws substantially on the company's and industry bodies' own figures — a different evidentiary standing from the government and specialist-report material elsewhere on this site.
  • Peer-reviewed and NGO literature on REIPPPP community trusts nationally: reviews finding trusts average a 9–12% equity stake and a projected R27.1 billion in community dividends over 20 years, alongside independent findings that implementation is uneven — compliance-driven in places, with slow payouts to residents (WWF-commissioned review of local community development in REIPPPP; academic literature on REIPPPP community ownership).
  • Suid-Afrika se Waterstofgemeenskap-padkaart (Departement van Wetenskap en Innovasie, 2021); die Groen Waterstof Kommersialiseringstrategie (2022); en die Regverdige Energie-oorgang Beleggingsplan 2023–2027 — die nasionale beleidsgrondslag vir die hantering van groen waterstof as 'n strategiese prioriteit, insluitend geprojekteerde nasionale uitstoot- en werksyfers.
  • SAPVIA, “Phelan Green Group Celebrates Public Health Breakthrough in De Aar Through Renewable Energy Investment” — en verwante bedryfs-/PR-dekking (pv magazine, SolarQuarter, Green Building Africa, PRWeb) van die De Aar- en Loeriesfontein-sonaanlegte se konstruksie-indiensneming en gemeenskapsontwikkeling-besteding. Onafhanklik gerapporteer, maar let op dit steun grootliks op die maatskappy en bedryfsliggame se eie syfers — 'n ander bewysstatus as die regerings- en spesialisverslag-materiaal elders op hierdie webwerf.
  • Eweknie-hersiende en NRO-literatuur oor REIPPPP-gemeenskapstrusts nasionaal: hersienings wat vind trusts hou gemiddeld 'n 9–12% aandeelbelang en 'n geprojekteerde R27.1 miljard in gemeenskapsdividende oor 20 jaar, saam met onafhanklike bevindinge dat implementering ongelyk is — nakomings-gedrewe op plekke, met stadige uitbetalings aan inwoners (WWF-aangevraagde hersiening van plaaslike gemeenskapsontwikkeling in REIPPPP; akademiese literatuur oor REIPPPP-gemeenskapsbesit).
